# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

SecurityCorp is a public SecurityCorp portfolio and cybersecurity knowledge
site. It presents security projects, guides, and future knowledge-base content
for a mixed technical audience while keeping public material sanitized and
evidence-backed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a Next.js static export built for Cloudflare Pages. Existing routes,
content hierarchy, keyboard behavior, reduced-motion behavior, responsive
layouts, and Three.js/CSS motion are incumbent product behavior. Repository
content remains internal-source until the publication-safety policy and human
approval gates are satisfied.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the deep-navy security-operations visual direction, cyberpunk/netrunner
character, restrained cyan/teal accents, tiger guardian imagery, existing
typography and spacing conventions, and the established interaction language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast, mobile and tablet
behavior, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives are mandatory. Do not make
functionality hover-only or desktop-only.
