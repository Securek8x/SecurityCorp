# Private editorial workspace

Put article briefs, research ledgers, outlines, drafts, lab records, and
review records for in-progress articles in a topic-specific subdirectory
here. This directory is deliberately outside `lib/knowledge-content.ts`,
the only published knowledge-content registry.

Files here are `internal-source` or `sanitized-draft` under
[`docs/publication-safety-policy.md`](../../docs/publication-safety-policy.md).
They are not routes, catalog cards, sitemap entries, RSS items, metadata, or
social-preview input. Do not add an article to the registry until the human
approval gate in the content workflow has been completed.

Start with the templates in
[`docs/content-workflow/templates/`](../../docs/content-workflow/templates/).
